# Legal rule sources for the photo inspection demo

Checked 18 September 2026. This is a source map for a limited SIH demo, not a complete statement of Indian packaging law. The Department of Consumer Affairs' [compiled 2011 rules](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf) include amendments through 2022. Read them with the later Gazette notifications linked below. A rule shown in the app should carry its rule number, amendment notification, effective date, source URL, and the category conditions used to apply it.

## Common declarations

For a retail package within Chapter II's scope, Rule 6(1) calls for a definite, plain, conspicuous declaration on the package or a securely attached label. The following are candidates for the first checklist, subject to the exceptions below. [Compiled rules, Rule 6](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf).

| Check | Source and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Manufacturer and packer names and addresses; importer name and address for an imported package | Rule 6(1)(a). Food articles follow the Food Safety and Standards Act and its rules for this clause. [Compiled Rule 6](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf); [G.S.R. 629(E), effective 1 January 2018](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/uploads/legal-metrology-acts-rules/8%28xii%29_0.pdf). |
| Country of origin, manufacture, or assembly for imported products | Rule 6(1)(aa), inserted by [G.S.R. 629(E), effective 1 January 2018](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/uploads/legal-metrology-acts-rules/8%28xii%29_0.pdf). Treat import status as an input to confirm, not a fact inferred solely from a photo. |
| Common or generic commodity name | Rule 6(1)(b). Multiple products in a package need their names and numbers or quantities. [Compiled Rule 6](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf). |
| Net quantity by standard unit of weight or measure, or number | Rule 6(1)(c). A photograph may verify the **declared text**, but cannot establish the package's actual contents. [Compiled Rule 6](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf); [Department advisory, 18 January 2023](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/2023.01.18%20advisory%20for%20outer%20gift%20package%20declarations.pdf). |
| Month and year | Rule 6(1)(d) concerns manufacture. [G.S.R. 779(E)](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2021/230946.pdf) removed the words "or pre-packed or imported" from that clause; [G.S.R. 226(E)](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/uploads/legal-metrology-acts-rules/GSR226.pdf) moved commencement to 1 October 2022. Food, seeds, cosmetics, bidis, incense sticks, and specified LPG packages have special provisions in [compiled Rule 6](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf). Do not implement a universal "manufactured/packed/imported date" rule from the problem statement alone. |
| Maximum retail price | Rule 6(1)(e) requires the maximum retail price inclusive of all taxes in Indian currency. The 2021 amendment changed the wording and removed the older examples; its start moved to 1 October 2022. Alcoholic beverages and specified essential commodities have special pricing provisions. [Compiled Rule 6](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf); [G.S.R. 779(E)](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2021/230946.pdf); [G.S.R. 226(E)](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/uploads/legal-metrology-acts-rules/GSR226.pdf). |
| Consumer care contact | Rule 6(2) covers the contact name and address, telephone number, and email address for consumer complaints. Check the rule text and any category law before treating a missing subfield as a violation. [Compiled Rule 6(2)](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf); [Department FAQ, questions 46-47](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_FAQs.pdf). |

The [Department's 2023 advisory](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/2023.01.18%20advisory%20for%20outer%20gift%20package%20declarations.pdf) also lists best-before/use-by dates when relevant, dimensions when relevant, and unit sale price. These need their own applicability rules if included. The first demo checklist should not silently treat them as passed. Rule 6(11)'s unit-price requirements were amended by [G.S.R. 226(E)](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/uploads/legal-metrology-acts-rules/GSR226.pdf).

## Mixed-retail conditions that matter

- Chapter II does not apply to packages above 25 kg or 25 L, specified cement, fertiliser, and farm produce bags above 50 kg, or packages meant for industrial or institutional consumers under Rule 3 as substituted by [G.S.R. 629(E), effective 1 January 2018](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/uploads/legal-metrology-acts-rules/8%28xii%29_0.pdf). The Department described a proposed change to the quantity exclusion in [August 2024](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/02-Aug-2024.pdf); a proposal is not an operative rule.
- Rule 26 contains further exemptions. A [2022 amendment, effective 1 January 2023](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2022/238255.pdf) addresses garments and hosiery sold loose or open. A [2025 amendment, effective 1 February 2026](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/2nd%20PCR%20Pan%20Masala_1764736734.pdf) removes the Rule 26(a) exemption for pan masala. Mixed retail needs category-specific applicability, including small packages.
- [G.S.R. 778(E), published 24 October 2025](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267107.pdf) sends declaration, numeral-height, and numeral-width requirements for packages containing medical devices to the Medical Devices Rules, 2017. The demo should mark those checks outside its covered rules unless it implements that separate law.
- Food articles have a separate regime for some declarations under [Rule 6(1)(a) and (d)](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf). The [Department FAQ, question 46](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_FAQs.pdf), says MRP, net weight, and consumer-care details for food remain under these packaged-commodity rules. Avoid a single all-fields checklist for food.
- Outer retail, grouped, gift, and promotional packages may need declarations on more than one package. See [Rule 4 as amended by G.S.R. 779(E)](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2021/230946.pdf) and the [Department's 18 January 2023 advisory](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/2023.01.18%20advisory%20for%20outer%20gift%20package%20declarations.pdf).
- [G.S.R. 418(E), published 1 June 2026](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/273053.pdf) clarifies that eligible importers may add declarations in specified bonded warehouses, but retail packages must bear them before leaving. This affects where in the supply chain a photo was taken. Its Rule 27 registration changes are outside this demo.
- E-commerce listing duties are separate from physical-label checks. [G.S.R. 312(E), dated 27 April 2026](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/272105.pdf) sets 1 July 2027 for a country-of-origin listing filter under Rule 6(10A). Do not add listing checks to a photograph inspection.

## Visibility, position, and type size

Rule 2(h) defines the principal display panel and allows required information to be grouped in one place or split between preprinted and online-printed information. Rule 9(1)(a) requires declarations to be legible and prominent. These provisions do **not** establish that every declaration must appear on the front face of a package. [Department explanation quoting Rules 2(h) and 9(1)(a)](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/Public%20invite%20upto%2031.8.2022.pdf). Multiple photographed sides and a user confirmation of coverage are needed before calling a declaration missing.

Rule 7 ties minimum numeral and letter height to the principal display panel's area, and has separate figures for moulded or formed text. It also specifies width relative to height. [G.S.R. 629(E), effective 1 January 2018](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/uploads/legal-metrology-acts-rules/8%28xii%29_0.pdf); [compiled Rule 7](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf). A photo with no physical scale cannot prove millimetre height or display-panel area. Even with a ruler, perspective and curved packaging may defeat measurement. Record "not assessed" when those inputs cannot be measured. Medical devices follow the separate 2025 rule above.

## Rule-record format for implementation

For each implemented rule, record `{ruleId, legalCitation, gazetteNumber, effectiveFrom, sourceUrl, appliesWhen, exceptions, checkMethod, reviewState}`. Do not use a single 2011 effective date for amended text. The app's "suspected violation" is a photo-based screening result; a team member should confirm the product category, imported status, photograph coverage, and the model's extracted text before export. These last workflow choices come from the demo scope, not the statute.
