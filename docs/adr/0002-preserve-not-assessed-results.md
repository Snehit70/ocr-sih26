# Preserve not-assessed results

The demo accepts mixed retail packages and multiple photographs per inspection. Its first rule set covers the common declaration groups in the problem statement: manufacturer, packer or importer name and address; net quantity; MRP; applicable month and year; and consumer care details. Date wording and category exceptions come from the reviewed rule source, not a universal manufacture, packing or import date check. A declaration may be outside the photographed views or a category may lack supported rules. A reviewer confirms that the relevant package sides were photographed before an unseen declaration can be marked missing. Font height also needs a known-size reference in the photograph before it can be checked in millimetres. Checks without enough evidence or supported rules remain "not assessed" rather than being treated as missing or compliant. This allows broad intake without claiming full legal coverage.
