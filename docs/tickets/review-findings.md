Part of #1. Depends on persistent photographs, structured observations, and rule results. A team member acts as reviewer in the SIH demo; no login or wizard is needed.

## Outcome

The reviewer can inspect evidence, correct model readings, decide suspected findings, and confirm an explainable report.

## Acceptance

- Show each observed declaration beside its source photograph and image region when available. Allow the reviewer to inspect the full photo.
- Let the reviewer correct extracted text and category. Preserve the original model observation and its source after correction.
- Ask the reviewer to confirm that relevant package sides were photographed before an unseen declaration can be called missing.
- Let the reviewer accept or reject each suspected violation and record the decision in the report.
- Keep an unreviewed report visibly draft. Confirmation records the reviewed result and time; exports use that confirmed state.
- Verify correction, photo-coverage gating, accepted and rejected findings, and reopening the confirmed report after reload.
