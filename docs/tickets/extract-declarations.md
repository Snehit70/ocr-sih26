Part of #1. Depends on the model connection and multi-photo inspection work. The model proposes observations; it does not decide legal compliance.

## Outcome

The model returns structured declarations and source references from the inspection's real photographs. The app can also suggest a broad category when the operator leaves category selection on Auto-detect.

## Acceptance

- Request the supported declaration groups from all photographs of one package and validate the response before using it.
- Preserve each observed value, source photo ID, image region when credible, and raw model output. An uncertain value or location remains unknown rather than becoming a fabricated field or highlight.
- Offer Auto-detect, Food and beverages, Personal care, Household products, and Other. A manual category selection takes precedence over the model suggestion, and the reviewer can correct it.
- Carry an imported or domestic indication as a suggestion for review, not as an unquestioned legal fact.
- Surface image-quality concerns so unreadable areas can be reviewed or recaptured.
- Verify multi-photo extraction, manual override, invalid output, and absent location with controlled model responses.
