Part of #1. Depends on photographs, model image references, and rule evaluation. The current font height and readability results are simulated from panel area or OCR text length.

## Outcome

Physical and visual checks say only what the photographs can support.

## Acceptance

- Replace simulated font height, contrast, and legibility claims with evidence-based results or not assessed.
- When a known-size reference and relevant panel measurements are usable, show the measurement method, estimated letter height, applicable cited threshold, and source photograph. If scale, perspective, curvature, or visibility prevents measurement, mark font height not assessed.
- Flag blur, crop, obstruction, or unreadable text for recapture or reviewer attention. A poor photograph cannot prove a declaration is missing.
- Show model-suggested text location only when the location can be checked against the photograph. Leave uncertain placement and potentially misleading wording to reviewer notes.
- Verify one measurable reference case and the unscaled or unreadable cases. No synthetic measurement may appear in the report.
