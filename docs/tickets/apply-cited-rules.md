Part of #1. Depends on structured observations. The current regex checks and weighted score can claim compliance even when evidence or applicability is uncertain.

## Outcome

Rules evaluate reviewed package facts and model observations without turning unsupported or unseen declarations into passes or failures.

## Acceptance

- Give each supported check an exact legal citation, source URL, effective date, applicability conditions, and expected declaration. Start with the [Department of Consumer Affairs' compiled rules](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf) and later [Gazette amendments](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267107.pdf); use the project's official-source note and have the team review the rule wording before the demo.
- Cover the agreed declaration groups: manufacturer, packer or importer name and address; net quantity; MRP; applicable date declaration; and consumer care details.
- Apply the date rule from its current source wording. The [2021 Gazette amendment](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2021/230946.pdf) removed "or pre-packed or imported" from Rule 6(1)(d); do not use a universal packing or import date check.
- Treat unsupported categories, inapplicable rules, poor evidence, and unconfirmed photo coverage as not assessed. A declaration becomes suspected missing only when the rule applies and the reviewer confirms sufficient photographed sides.
- Use the agreed report headlines. Keep any numeric score for internal testing only; it must not decide the report's legal wording.
- Verify applicability and missing-versus-not-assessed cases, including mixed-retail exceptions in the source note.
